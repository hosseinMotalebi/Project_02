Italy in 21 Days: A Journey Through Art, History, and Culture

Embark on a 21-day Italian adventure that takes you from the bustling streets of Rome to the serene landscapes of Tuscany, the romantic canals of Venice, and beyond. With ample time in each destination, this trip offers a perfect balance of exploration, relaxation, and cultural immersion.

Itinerary
  • 1
    Day 1 to 4: Rome
    • Colosseum, Roman Forum, Palatine Hill
    • Vatican Museums, St. Peter's Basilica, Sistine Chapel
    • Trevi Fountain, Piazza Navona, Trastevere
    • Jewish Ghetto dinner
  • 2
    Day 5 to 10: Florence
    • Uffizi Gallery, Accademia (David)
    • Florence Cathedral (Duomo), Piazzale Michelangelo
    • Day Trips: Pisa, Siena, Chianti (wine tasting)
  • 3
    Day 11 to 12: Venice
    • Gondola ride, St. Mark's Basilica, Doge's Palace
    • Rialto Bridge, local markets
    • Murano (glass), Burano (colorful houses)
  • 4
    Day 13 to 15: Milan
    • Duomo, Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II
    • The Last Supper, Sforza Castle
    • Day Trip: Lake Como
  • 5
    Day 16 to 18: Naples
    • Spaccanapoli, Naples Cathedral, pizza tasting
    • Pompeii ruins, Archaeological Museum
    • Sunset at Castel dell'Ovo
  • 6
    Day 18 to 20: Amalfi Coast
    • Positano, Amalfi, Ravello
    • Boat trip to Capri, Blue Grotto, Anacapri
